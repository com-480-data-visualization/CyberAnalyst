# CyberAnalyst — Screencast Script

**Duration:** 2 minutes max
**URL:** https://com-480-data-visualization.github.io/CyberAnalyst/

**Story:** The world is at war → find your country → it's closer than you think.

---

## Act 1 — "The world is at war" (~40s)

**Action:** Page is loaded. Move the cursor slowly across the background — let the nodes dodge away. Click once to spawn a pulse ring.

**Say:**
> "There's been a cyber arms race for 20 years. 6,000 documented incidents. 160 countries. We made it readable."

**Action:** Scroll down to the streamgraph. Let the streams animate in. Don't explain it — go straight to the interaction.

**Say:**
> "This is every documented cyberattack for the past 6 years. Watch what happens in February 2022."

**Action:** Click the Exploitation toggle to hide it. The Disruption band visibly doubles.

**Say:**
> "Russia invades Ukraine — and immediately, the cyber front explodes. This isn't a coincidence."

**Action:** Click one of the amber pins near the Feb 2022 spike to show the event description.

**Say:**
> "Every one of these pins marks a moment where world events and cyber warfare collided."

---

## Act 2 — "Find your country" (~40s)

**Action:** Scroll to the globe. Drag to rotate it toward Europe.

**Say:**
> "But who's actually being targeted? Spin the globe."

**Action:** Click Ukraine. Show the threat actor card.

**Say:**
> "Ukraine — mostly Russian state actors. Disruption, infrastructure attacks."

**Action:** Click the Exploitation filter button. Rotate toward the US. Click it.

**Say:**
> "Switch to espionage. Now China dominates. Different war, same battlefield."

---

## Act 3 — "It's closer than you think" (~35s)

**Action:** Scroll to the network graph.

**Say:**
> "You might think Switzerland is safe — neutral, tucked away. It isn't."

**Action:** Click the Cyber Fraud node.

**Say:**
> "84% of all reported cybercrime here is financial fraud. Phishing, investment scams, romance scams. Click any node and the full picture emerges."

**Action:** Click the Phishing leaf node to highlight the path.

**Action:** Scroll to the age chart. Click the Phishing segment on the 35–44 bar.

**Say:**
> "And who gets hit? 35 to 44 year olds — old enough to have savings, online enough to be targeted. That might be you. That might be your parents."

**Action:** Click to reset. Let the full chart show.

**Say:**
> "From geopolitics to your inbox — this is CyberAnalyst."

---

## Tips

- Record at 1080p, full screen browser, zoom to ~90%
- Pause 1 second before each interaction so the viewer can follow
- Deliver it with energy — the data is dramatic, match it
- Keep it under 2 minutes — the graders are strict on this
